Affiliate marketing has a reputation problem. And honestly, it's earned.

Most affiliate programmes exist to incentivise people to promote products they don't use, don't believe in, and wouldn't recommend to anyone they actually cared about. The recommendation is transactional. The endorsement is purchased. The person receiving the recommendation is a vehicle for commission, not someone whose life might actually improve.

Dog and Bone's affiliate programme is built on a different premise: **the best advertising we will ever have is a person who switched to a Dog and Bone phone, genuinely improved their life, and told someone they cared about.**

That person is going to recommend us anyway. Our affiliate programme just means we can say thank you in a way that puts money in their pocket.

## How the Programme Works

The mechanics are straightforward.

When you sign up as a Dog and Bone affiliate, you receive a unique referral link and code. When someone uses your link or code to purchase a Dog and Bone phone, you earn a commission on their order.

**You earn 15% of every sale you refer.** Dog and Bone phones typically sell for £150–£200, which means a typical successful referral earns you £22–£30.

There is no cap on earnings. No minimum referrals required to receive payment. No complex tiered structure to navigate. You refer someone, they buy a phone, you get paid.

Payouts are processed monthly by bank transfer or PayPal, with a minimum payout threshold of £20.

## Who Earns the Most

The people who earn most from the Dog and Bone affiliate programme are, reliably, the people who are most genuine about their recommendation.

This is not accidental — it's the predictable result of people being able to tell the difference between a genuine endorsement and a promotional post. The person who posts about their Dog and Bone phone because they genuinely love it, who answers questions honestly, who talks about the adjustment period as well as the benefits, converts far better than the person who produces a polished promotional post because they want the commission.

Our best affiliates include:

**Parents** who gave their child a Family profile phone and share their experience in parenting forums and Facebook groups. The emotional resonance of a genuinely improved family life is extremely persuasive to other parents dealing with the same problem.

**Adult children** who bought a Senior profile for an elderly parent and described the change in their parent's relationship with technology. The relief these purchases provide to families is something people want to share.

**Digital wellness advocates** — people who write, create content, or simply talk a lot about intentional technology use — who found Dog and Bone consistent with what they already believed and were recommending.

**People who switched personally** and talked honestly about the adjustment and the transformation. First-person authenticity is the most persuasive thing there is.

## What You Don't Have to Do

We want to be explicit about what our affiliate programme does not ask of you.

You do not have to pretend the transition is easy. It isn't always. The first week with a phone that doesn't have Instagram is uncomfortable for many people. Honest affiliates talk about this, and it makes their eventual endorsement more credible, not less.

You do not have to promote the product to people it isn't right for. Dog and Bone is not for everyone. It is for people who genuinely want to reduce their phone use. If someone tells you they love their smartphone and are happy with it, they are not your audience. Don't waste your relationship currency on a hard sell.

You do not have to create content if you don't want to. Many affiliates earn commission simply by having their referral link in their email signature, mentioning it in relevant conversations, or sharing it when they're directly asked what phone they recommend.

You do not have to be a content creator, influencer, or have a large following. Our best-earning affiliates are sometimes people with small, highly engaged audiences where their recommendation carries enormous weight — not accounts with millions of followers and low conversion.

## The Part That Matters Most

There's something that feels qualitatively different about recommending Dog and Bone compared to other affiliate programmes.

When you tell someone about a credit card or a protein powder or a software subscription, you might earn a commission and they might get something they find useful. That's a transaction.

When you tell a parent that their teenager doesn't have to live with anxiety and comparison and the social performance stress of Instagram — and that there's a practical, affordable solution available right now — you're doing something different. You're potentially changing the course of a child's mental health trajectory during a formative period.

When you help an adult child finally give their elderly parent a phone they're not frightened of, you're restoring a person's confidence and dignity.

When you help someone who's been struggling with their own phone use for years actually fix the problem — not with another self-discipline hack but with a structural change — you're giving them back time and peace of mind they thought were gone.

**This is affiliate marketing for good.** We pay you because you're helping people. The money is the secondary thing. The primary thing is that somewhere, a family is having better dinners, a teenager is sleeping better, an elderly person is confidently video-calling their grandchildren, or an adult has discovered they have three hours a day they didn't know they had.

That is what you're facilitating when you refer someone to Dog and Bone.

- Your name and email address
