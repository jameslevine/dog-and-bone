There's a moment most of us have had. You pick up your phone to check the weather. Forty-five minutes later, you've watched three videos about a topic you don't care about, scrolled through photographs of people you barely know, and read an argument between strangers on the internet. The weather remains unchecked. The moment you needed your phone for has passed.

That moment is not an accident. It is the product.

## The Industry Built to Steal Your Attention

Modern smartphones are not communication devices with a distraction problem. They are attention-harvesting machines with a communication feature bolted on. Every notification badge, every infinite scroll, every autoplay video, every algorithmically tuned feed has been designed by some of the most talented engineers and psychologists in the world with one singular goal: keeping you looking at the screen for longer.

The business model is simple. **Your attention is the product.** Advertisers pay for eyeballs. The platforms compete for your time. And the more time you spend, the more money they make. This is not a conspiracy theory — it is openly described in their investor presentations.

The result is a device that most people feel controlled by, rather than in control of. A 2023 study by the American Psychological Association found that 74% of Americans believe smartphones have made them less focused, less present, and more anxious. Yet the average person still spends four to six hours per day on their phone.

We knew all of this. We talked about it. We read the books. We downloaded the screen time apps. We set limits on Instagram. We put our phones in drawers at dinner.

And yet nothing really changed.

## The Realisation

The founders of Dog and Bone came at this problem from different directions, but arrived at the same conclusion: **the problem isn't willpower. The problem is the phone itself.**

No amount of digital self-discipline can reliably overcome an interface specifically designed to defeat it. Telling someone to resist a slot machine that lives in their pocket is not a solution. The slot machine needs to be redesigned.

We looked around for phones that prioritised simplicity. There were dumb phones — old-style devices with number keypads and no apps. They solved the distraction problem but created new ones: no maps, no camera, no messaging apps your family already uses. Going back to 2003 is not a realistic answer for most people.

There were some "minimalist" Android launchers — apps that stripped back the home screen. But the social media apps, the notification systems, the dopamine loops — they were all still there, one swipe away. The temptation was always present, just hidden behind a thin veneer.

What we wanted didn't exist: **a smartphone that does everything you genuinely need, and none of what you don't.** A phone with maps and camera and WhatsApp and banking — but without Instagram, without TikTok, without the infinite scroll, without the algorithmic feeds designed to make you feel bad so you'll keep scrolling.

So we built it.

## What Dog and Bone Actually Is

Dog and Bone is a Samsung Galaxy A12 — a reliable, well-made Android smartphone with a great camera, long battery life, and wide availability of spare parts — that we configure at the operating system level before it arrives at your door.

We don't just uninstall the bad apps. We configure Android to **prevent those apps from being reinstalled.** We remove the app store access that would let someone undo the configuration in a weak moment. We set up the launcher to show only the apps that matter.

The result is a phone that feels fundamentally different to use. There's nothing pulling you back. No notifications from apps you didn't choose. No red badges. No suggested content. Just the tools you asked for, working quietly, waiting to be used when you need them.

We offer three profiles:

**The Senior profile** is built for older users who want to stay connected to family without the complexity of a modern smartphone. Large text, simple layout, the apps they need, nothing they don't.

**The Family profile** is designed for children and teenagers. It provides communication, maps, and music — but no social media, no YouTube, no app store. Parents control what's on the phone from the start.

**The Balance profile** is for adults who know they're spending too much time on their phones but can't break the habit with willpower alone. It keeps your banking, maps, messaging and camera, but removes the platforms designed to exploit your attention.

## The Philosophy

We believe technology should serve humans, not the other way around. **A good tool does its job and stays out of the way.** A hammer doesn't try to convince you to keep hammering when you've finished the shelf. A telephone doesn't try to keep you on the line after you've finished the call.

Somewhere along the way, our phones stopped being tools and became environments. Places we live in, rather than things we use. And the people who designed those environments did not have your wellbeing in mind.

Dog and Bone is our attempt to change that. Not by going backwards to a simpler time, but by taking the best parts of what a smartphone can do — connecting people, helping with navigation, capturing memories, enabling work — and building a wall around everything else.

It's called Dog and Bone because in Cockney rhyming slang, that's what a phone is. It's a nod to our British roots, a reminder that a phone is just a phone, and a small bit of joy in a product category that has become very serious and very stressful.

A simple phone. A happy life.
